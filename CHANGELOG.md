# Terminal Manager — What's New

The latest updates to Terminal Manager, in plain language. Newest first.

## 1.1.0: Telegram notifications (16 November 2026)

Hear about your terminals on your phone.

- **Telegram notifications (optional, off by default).** Connect your own Telegram bot in Settings, and Terminal Manager tells you when a terminal stops, runs again or loses its connection. You can also ask it for the status of your terminals from Telegram. When you connect it, Terminal Manager also gives the bot its photo, name and descriptions. You can turn it off at any time.
- **Read-only mode.** In this version Terminal Manager does not change your strategies, charts, EA files or trading settings, and deploying is not available. Charts you have already deployed stay on your terminals and keep running. If you need these before the next version, you can keep using Terminal Manager 1.0.
- **A notice when Windows runs out of desktop memory.** Home shows an estimate of how many more terminals Windows can open and an Increase it button. Nothing changes unless you press the button.
- **Account details follow Account monitoring.** With Account monitoring on, Terminal Manager reads the account details as soon as you switch it on or add a terminal, and tells you when a terminal has no broker or account number.
- **Scanner shows what is running.** The Scanner tab has All, Running and Not running filters with counts.
- **A clearer update banner** for anyone who leaves the preview channel.
- **Many smaller fixes.**

The full notes for this version, including the Schedule A revision for Terminal Manager 1.1, are on the [1.1.0 release](../../releases/tag/v1.1.0).

## 1.0.0 — First public release

Terminal Manager goes public — free for everyone.

- **Find every terminal in one place.** Scan for MetaTrader 5 installations and manage them from a single window.

- **Deployment History.** A traceable record of what was deployed to which terminal, and when.
- **Automatic updates**, with an optional preview channel in Settings.
